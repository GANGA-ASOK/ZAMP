Kumar Digital — PAN card is a screenshot of someone else's card
Expected: review

The uploaded 'PAN card' is a low-resolution screenshot from a social-media video showing an individual's card (RAHUL KUMAR, ABCPK1234F). It can never be approved: when OCR cannot read it the case goes to review and the vendor is asked for a clear copy of the company's own PAN card; when OCR does read it, the PAN matches neither the form nor the GSTIN (AAHCK4471B) and is an individual's 'P' PAN, which escalates it to reject.
